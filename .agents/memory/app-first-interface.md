---
name: App-first reporting interface
description: TrafficSignal's task-focused, mobile-first experience and scope constraint
---

Treat TrafficSignal as a city services application, not a city information or marketing website. Residents should be able to immediately choose reporting or their report history, then work through focused screens.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requested a mobile-first application experience without large hero sections, promotional sections, excessive scrolling, or a traditional website navigation pattern.

**How to apply:** Keep navigation persistent, reporting guided, and each screen focused on a resident task. Preserve the prototype's branding, current colors, stored data, database connections, AI features, and existing functionality when changing the interface.

Keep the public accident map and accident list under one Accidents navigation entry, rather than separate primary tabs.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requested combining the incident map and accidents tabs.

**How to apply:** Treat Map and List as views of the same public-accidents feature when extending navigation. Preserve both views' existing capabilities and keep private resident report history separate.
