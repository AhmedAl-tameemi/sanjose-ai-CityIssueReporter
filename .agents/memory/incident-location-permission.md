---
name: Incident location permission
description: Purpose and privacy boundary for permission-assisted resident location.
---

Resident location permission is for suggesting where an incident happened, not for monitoring residents' movements. Keep it optional and use a one-time location reading rather than background tracking.

**Why:** The user requested permission-assisted precise incident location and automatic selection. A one-time reading supports that purpose without collecting ongoing movement data.

**How to apply:** Preserve a manual address/map-pin alternative and let residents verify or adjust any device-location suggestion before submitting. Do not introduce continuous tracking without a new, explicit requirement and consent flow.
