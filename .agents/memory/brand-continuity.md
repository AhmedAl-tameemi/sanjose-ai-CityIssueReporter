---
name: Brand continuity
description: The identity constraint for TrafficSignal interface improvements
---

Retain TrafficSignal's logo, brand name, and teal/yellow identity when improving its interface. The owner subsequently requested softer pastel colors and a clean look, so lighter mint and lemon surfaces are an approved refinement of that identity.

**Why:** The project owner explicitly asked for a clearer, more polished interface using the existing brand, not a rebrand. The first very pale teal was later judged too faint; the requested balance is darker pastel teal, but not a dark teal layout.

**How to apply:** Reuse the existing theme and brand assets, favoring visibly tinted pastel surfaces with readable dark teal text and actions. Do not interpret brand continuity as requiring the earlier saturated colors or nearly white teal.

Keep Report History visually secondary to the logo, with a compact button and clearly separated header placement.

**Why:** The owner repeated the request for a smaller button farther from the TrafficSignal logo after the first adjustment was insufficient.

**How to apply:** Preserve the compact hierarchy and generous logo-to-control spacing in future header changes, including mobile layouts.
